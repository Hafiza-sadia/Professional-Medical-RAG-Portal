💡 Empirical Insights

Key Scientific Observations

📐 Optimal Chunking Granularity

Medium-sized chunks (600 characters with 120 character overlap) strike the ideal balance in clinical documents. Coarse chunks (1000+ chars) dilute semantic similarity and introduce irrelevant context, while ultra-short chunks (300 chars) fragment diagnostic criteria lists, lowering single-passage precision.

🛡️ Zero-Hallucination Abstention

By enforcing a normalized cosine similarity threshold of 0.35, MediQ achieved 100% Abstention Accuracy on negative, out-of-domain, and non-medical queries. Instead of fabricating plausible-sounding clinical advice, the system strictly disclaims evidence absence.

⚡ Real-Time Clinical Latency

Embedding retrieval across all four configurations consistently executed in 24–32 milliseconds on CPU. This demonstrates that normalized FAISS vector indexing provides sub-50ms clinical retrieval suitable for high-concurrency healthcare portals.
